### A2 input

“Requirements: match the event window and upload originals. Architecture: an iPhone task fires exactly ten minutes after the event and uploads everything in one request. Failure Modes: retry any failed request forever. Tradeoffs: not sure. Verification: open the app once and check photos.”

### A2 assistant response

Your requirements establish two useful checks: select photos against the approved event window and preserve originals. “Tradeoffs: not sure” is a complete first attempt; you can work through that in review.

The consequential gaps are:

- **Timing and recovery.** Your architecture depends on execution at an exact moment. We have no platform evidence establishing that guarantee. If execution is delayed or interrupted, the upload could be missed. The concept is separating the desired schedule from a recoverable workflow. Would you revise the timing promise and explain how unfinished work resumes? The specific iOS execution guarantees still need verification against platform documentation.
- **One request and unlimited retries.** If the connection fails after the backend accepts some work, the client may not know what succeeded. Retrying the whole request could repeat work; retrying every error forever could also repeat a permanently invalid request. The concepts are durable progress, safe repetition, and distinguishing recoverable failures from permanent ones. Reconsider what progress must survive an interruption and when retries should stop.
- **Tradeoffs at one-user scale.** Simplicity is a reasonable aim, but the first attempt leaves the reliability-versus-complexity choice unexplained. Describe the smallest amount of recovery machinery you think this use warrants.
- **Verification.** Opening the app once checks one successful path. It does not establish recovery after interruption, correct selection at the event boundaries, or preservation of originals. What evidence would let you trust those outcomes?

The photo permission scope is a missing fact, so the set of photos the app can actually access remains unresolved. I haven’t chosen a permission model or a replacement architecture for you.

Revise or confirm your choices in one pass. You can leave a question explicitly unresolved.

### A3 input

“I will persist per-event/per-photo progress, treat background timing as best effort, catch up on foreground, deduplicate uploads, bound retries and test interruption. Keep it one-user simple; no new queue service. I confirm those choices; unknown photo permission scope stays unresolved.”

Engineering judgment is **completed**. Here is the handoff to the interview workflow.
